Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Winter Score LLC (“Winter Score,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with www.winterscore.com and related professional inquiries.

Winter Score LLC is a Wyoming limited liability company. Our principal office is 30 N Gould St Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA. You can contact us at privacy@winterscore.com.

The website is designed as a simple composer portfolio. It does not currently use contact forms, user accounts, payment processing, advertising pixels, or analytics cookies.

We may receive personal information if you choose to contact us directly by email or another linked platform. That information may include your name, email address, company, project details, and any other information you choose to provide.

We use information to respond to inquiries, evaluate potential projects, communicate about professional services, operate and secure the website, maintain business records, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for targeted advertising. We may share information with service providers who help operate the website, email, hosting, security, file storage, or business administration, including our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, Inc., and only as reasonably necessary for those services.

We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, professional advisors, or to protect our rights, safety, business, website, or users.

5. Cookies, Analytics, and Tracking

The website does not currently set analytics or advertising cookies. Because we do not track visitors across third-party websites, the website does not currently respond differently to browser “Do Not Track” signals or Global Privacy Control signals.

Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may set limited technical cookies necessary for security, performance, and bot protection. These are not used by Winter Score LLC for advertising or behavioral tracking. If we add analytics or tracking in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

10. Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided to us, subject to reasonable identity verification and any legal or business recordkeeping obligations.
