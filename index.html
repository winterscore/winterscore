Sebastian Winter

Composer

Sebastian Winter is a composer based between Los Angeles and New York whose work fuses orchestral writing with cutting-edge sound design to create music of cinematic scope. A graduate of The Juilliard School, where he studied under Academy Award-winning composer John Corigliano, his music has been performed in venues such as Carnegie Hall, and he has previously worked at Hans Zimmer’s Remote Control Productions in Santa Monica.

Winter’s credits include major studio films such as The Conjuring: Last Rites (Warner Bros.), Predator: Killer of Killers (20th Century Studios), Alien: Romulus (20th Century Studios), Kraven the Hunter (Sony), Twisters (Universal), and The Flash (Warner Bros.). Alongside these projects, his original scores showcase a style built for scale, drama, and cinematic grandeur.
